— About KAZ —

Where Every Detail
Is Crafted With Care

KAZ Sushi & Japanese Fusion Restaurant has quickly become one of Addis Ababa's most highly-rated destinations for fine dining. Whether it's a romantic dinner date, an intimate celebration, or a gathering with close friends, our space is designed to make every moment feel effortless and unforgettable.

From meticulously prepared sushi to bold Pan-Asian flavors, our kitchen blends authentic Japanese technique with a modern fusion sensibility — served with immaculate, attentive hospitality in an ambiance that feels both intimate and elevated.

📍

Conveniently located by Friendship and the new Arada Park.
